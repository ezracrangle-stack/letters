About

I'm Ezra Crangle. I write Ezra's Notes, a newsletter on value investing. Fund letters are one of the best free sources in this business: a good place to find investment ideas and to see how professional investors think. They're scattered across dozens of websites, so I put the ones I read in one place.

The list of managers and the comments are mine. Links go to the manager's own site. When a firm no longer hosts an old letter, I link to a public copy and mark it. Letters meant only for a fund's investors stay out.

It's free and it stays free. No account, no email gate, no paywall. Most sites that collect fund letters ask you to sign up or pay first. I'd rather you spend that minute reading.

Broken links and corrections

Found a broken link, a letter filed under the wrong date or a company in the wrong place? If you manage a fund and want your letters removed or linked differently, the same applies. Write to letters [at] ezrasnotes [dot] com and I'll fix it.
