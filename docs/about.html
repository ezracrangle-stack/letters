About

I'm Ezra. I write Ezra's Notes, a newsletter on value investing. Fund letters are one of the best free sources in this business, and they're scattered across dozens of websites. So I put the ones I read in one place.

The list of managers and the comments are mine. Once a week a small script visits each manager's letters page and adds anything new. To build the company index it reads each new letter once and keeps only the company names it finds. It never copies or re-hosts a letter. Links go to the manager's own site. When a firm no longer hosts an old letter, I link to a public copy and mark it. Letters meant only for a fund's investors stay out.

It's free and it stays free. No account, no email gate, no paywall. Most sites that collect fund letters ask you to sign up or pay first. I'd rather you spend that minute reading.

Looking for a letter?

If there's a letter or a manager you'd like to see here, write to me at letters [at] ezrasnotes [dot] com. If it's published openly, I'll add it.

For fund managers

The script identifies itself as EzrasNotesLettersBot, follows your robots.txt, loads your letters page once a week and opens each new letter once. If you'd like your letters removed or linked differently, write to letters [at] ezrasnotes [dot] com and I'll fix it.
