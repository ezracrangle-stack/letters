Abdulaziz Alnaim

Mayar Capital

Responsible global value: quality businesses bought at a discount, with ethical and Shariah screens. London-based, letters since 2011.

Best for Global value investors, especially those who invest with ethical or Shariah screens.

Why I read Abdulaziz

Aziz has written to his partners every quarter since 2011, including the long stretch when value lagged and a few expensive tech stocks led the index. His letters show a global value investor holding his ground and explaining why.
